    The Business Problem

    Automated GST Return & ITC Compliance Suite (GSTR-1, 2B, 3B, 7, 8, 9, 10)

    The Business Problem

    Tax and compliance teams struggled to cross-verify outward supplies, auto-drafted Input Tax Credit (ITC), TDS/TCS filings, and annual returns across fragmented raw filing exports.

    Process & Architecture

    First cleaned, normalized, and validated raw filing tables in SQL to generate a unified metadata schema; built statistical validation reports in SAS (v3.5/4.0) and interactive Power BI dashboards covering GSTR-1, 2B, 3B, 7, 8, 9, 10, ITC claim mismatches, and turnover suppression indicators.

    Key Insight & Result

    Accelerated multi-return reconciliation cycles and surfaced high-priority ITC discrepancies and turnover variances before compliance deadlines.
